# Cinematic Car Commercial: "The Snap"

Video-generation prompt. References: `@image1` = subject, `@image2` = vehicle.

## Format

Vertical 9:16, 13 s, one continuous locked-off shot with no cuts. Photorealistic cinematic car commercial, 24 fps look.

## Subject (@image1)

Keep the face from @image1 exactly: short curly hair, salt-and-pepper beard, black sunglasses, gold chains, shirtless as in the reference. The subject sits centered on a low black folding camping chair with knees apart and feet flat. The left hand rests on the lap. The right hand is raised at shoulder height, thumb and middle finger pressed together, ready to snap. The expression is calm and deadpan, eyes locked straight on the lens the whole time.

## Vehicle (@image2)

Keep the red Tesla Model Y from @image2 exactly: same body, paint, wheels, lights and badges. Its nose always points straight at the camera, and the car stays on the camera's center axis.

## Location

A flat high-desert plain of packed ochre dirt with scattered pebbles and sparse dry scrub. Low blue-grey mountains sit on the horizon under a pale sky with one thin band of cloud. Golden-hour backlight, an amber glow in the dust, long soft shadows falling toward the camera.

## Camera

Static tripod at the seated subject's chest height. Centered, symmetrical framing with an ~100 mm telephoto look. The compression stacks the background car right behind and above the subject. Shallow depth of field: the subject is tack sharp and the background falls soft. Subtle film grain, a cool sky against warm ground.

## Spatial layout (keep it consistent)

- Camera → subject: about 4 m.
- Launch rise: about 18 m behind the chair.
- Landing point: about 5 m behind the chair, centered behind the subject.
- The car comes straight down the center line from the far distance. It never reaches or passes the chair.

## Timeline

| Time | Speed | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.4 s | Real time | The subject is motionless with the hand raised. Far in the distance the red Model Y races straight toward camera, throwing rooster tails of dust to both sides and getting bigger in frame. |
| 2.4 s | — | One crisp finger snap. The hand freezes in place, still raised. At the same instant the car hits the small rise well behind the chair and launches, nose lifting. |
| 2.4–5.2 s | Smooth ramp to 25 % slow motion | The car floats in the air, framed behind and above the subject’s head. All four wheels are off the ground and the suspension hangs at full extension. Dust and pebbles hang in the air, backlit gold. The subject stays perfectly still. |
| 5.2–6.0 s | Ramp back to real time | The car lands hard on all four wheels in the open ground behind the chair. The suspension compresses, a burst of dust kicks up, and the car brakes to a full stop with the DRLs glowing. Its whole body stays visible and it never touches the subject. |
| 6.0–13.0 s | Real time | Warm dust drifts around the car, which stays visible the whole time. At ~7 s the subject slowly lowers the right hand to the lap, still looking into the lens. The dust settles and the final frame holds. |

## Avoid

Camera movement, shake, zoom, cuts. Changes to the face, hair, outfit or car. The subject flinching, blinking hard or turning their head. The car passing over, beside or into the subject, or landing on the chair. The car turning or showing its side. Extra people, vehicles or animals. Text, logos added, watermarks. Morphing, warping, duplicated wheels. Distorted hands or extra fingers.

## Notes

- The 2.4–5.2 s slow-motion window at 25 % covers only ~0.7 s of real airtime. A real car at speed covers 10+ m in that time, so the launch point sits far back (~18 m) and the car lands short of the chair. Otherwise the model tends to fly the car over the subject.
- Many generators cap a clip at 8–10 s. If yours does, generate 0–8 s and extend from the last frame for the 8–13 s dust-settling hold, since the camera is locked off.
